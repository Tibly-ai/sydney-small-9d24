About Sydney

A Voice Born for Low Light

Sydney Small sings the songs that live between midnight and last call — velvet-toned jazz standards, soulful originals, and torch songs that turn any room into a supper club.

The Long Way To The Mic

Sydney Small grew up on a steady diet of her grandmother's record collection — Ella, Nina, Sarah Vaughan spinning on Sunday afternoons in a Queens walk-up. She didn't chase the spotlight so much as grow into it, singing in church choirs and school talent shows before finding her way, guitar case in hand, to the open mic nights of the Lower East Side.

What started as a handful of songs at a corner bar became a decade-long love affair with New York City itself. Sydney has spent years learning the city one room at a time — the hush of a candlelit piano bar, the roar of a rooftop crowd, the intimacy of a Sunday brunch set where every regular knows her by name.

Today she's a fixture of the city's late-night circuit, known for turning any space — however small, however grand — into somewhere you don't want to leave.

Her Sound

A voice that moves fluidly between eras and genres — always smoky, always sincere.

Jazz & Standards

Reimagined classics from the Great American Songbook, delivered with a smoky alto and a taste for slow, deliberate phrasing.

Soul & Torch Song

Rich, emotive vocals built for stripped-down piano ballads and slow-burning love songs that hold a room in silence.

Original Material

Sydney's own songwriting draws on the city's neon and shadow — melancholic, cinematic, unmistakably New York.

Her Journey Across NYC

2015

First Open Mics, Lower East Side

2018

Residency, Village Piano Bar

Landed a weekly residency in Greenwich Village, sharpening a sound built for close, candlelit rooms.

2021

Rooftop & Private Circuit

Expanded into private events and rooftop lounges across Manhattan and Brooklyn, singing for weddings, galas, and late-night soirées.
